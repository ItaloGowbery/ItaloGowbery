---

### 🔬 What I'm working on

**[Smart CNC Reader](https://github.com/ItaloGowbery/Smart-CNC-Reader)** — a two-axis CNC instrument for automated spectral reading of well plates.
Built around an ESP32-S3 and an AS7341 11-channel spectral sensor, with a TFT status display and a web interface. Mechanical design in FreeCAD, 3D-printed parts, V-slot frame, and firmware in C++/PlatformIO. It replaces manual well-by-well reading at a fraction of the cost of a commercial reader.
